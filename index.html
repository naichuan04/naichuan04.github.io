Selected Publications and Projects

Precise Positioning of Micro Manipulation Tools Based on Super-Resolution

Naichuan Sun, Yue Lu, Mingzhu Sun†

44th Chinese Control Conference (CCC), 2025

We propose a micropipette localization method that combines multiple blur scale edge detection with LIIF super-resolution, using the centroid of tip-edge pixels to estimate the tip position.

Building a Mobile Household Service Robot

Team Leader · Robocompass, Nankai University

Mar. 2023 – Feb. 2024

We built a wheeled robotic platform equipped with a gripper and LiDAR, using YOLO, SLAM, and PID control to perform locomotion and manipulation tasks. Our team won the Space Service Competition at the 2024 World Robot Contest Championship.
